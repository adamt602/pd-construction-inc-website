PRIVACY

This policy explains how PD Construction Inc. collects and uses the information you submit through this website, including project inquiries sent through our contact form. We keep it brief and we keep it accurate.

Information We Collect

Only what you choose to send us: your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, the project type you select, and the details you write in the message field. We do not run advertising trackers on this site and we do not sell or share your information with third parties.

How We Use Information

We use it to respond to your inquiry, to prepare an estimate or schedule a site walk if the project moves forward, and to keep an internal record of the conversation. Inquiries are handled by the owners of the firm. If you would like your information removed from our records, write to us and we will delete it.

Form Submissions

Contact form submissions are delivered to us by email through a third-party form service, which processes the message solely to transmit it. No project details are published on this site without the owner’s permission.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to diane@pdconstructioninc.com.
